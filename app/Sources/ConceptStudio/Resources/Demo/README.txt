ConceptStudio offline demo, updated 2026-10-16.

These 24 photographs show the same fictional adult. They are synthetic, made for this application's
instructional walkthrough; no real person's identity or personal photos were supplied.

How they were made:
- window.jpg was rendered with Qwen-Image-2.1 and the LightX2V Lightning LoRA (4 steps, image editing),
  using a crop of the approved Review screen design comp as its reference.
- The other 23 were edits of window.jpg with the same model. Each prompt asked for the same woman in a
  different outfit, place and light, and only one person in the photo.

They are synthetic training examples. They are not output from a LoRA trained by ConceptStudio, and
they are not a dataset-quality experiment.

The app uses prepared per-image captions (DemoProject.preparedCaptions), prepared dataset advice and
simulated service outcomes. No captioning model, dataset upload, GPU rental or training run occurs in
demo mode.

Fonts/UbuntuSans-Bold.ttf is the trigger's display face. It is distributed under the Ubuntu Font
Licence 1.0 (Fonts/UFL-1.0.txt).
