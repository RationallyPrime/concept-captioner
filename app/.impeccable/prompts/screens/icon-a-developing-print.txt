A macOS app icon for ConceptStudio, a Mac app that turns a folder of photos into a trained image-model adapter. 1024 by 1024, the standard macOS rounded-square (squircle) icon shape on a transparent background with the usual inner margin, soft natural depth and a gentle top light, no text, no letters, no logo type. It must stay legible at 16 pixels. Design language "Instant Print": film-back charcoal (#282828), warm instant-print white paper, and a single developing-orange accent (#F86319) used sparingly. No photographic face, no camera, no lens, no robot, no sparkles, no gradient mesh.
Direction A, "Developing print": the squircle is charcoal. A single white instant print sits slightly forward of centre, square photo window above a deeper bottom margin. Inside the window, a simple, flat head-and-shoulders silhouette is half developed: the upper half crisp and light, the lower half still dissolving into charcoal grain. In the bottom margin, one short rounded orange bar, like a highlighted trigger word.
