# Regularization images

These pictures are regularization images: ordinary examples of the subject's class, drawn by the base model and trained beside the real subject so the class keeps its general meaning while the trigger learns one subject. **This job has no trigger and none is configured.** The job configuration lists the `class_nouns` of the subjects the pictures may show.

- Wherever the policy and the profile above say to use the trigger, write the subject's class noun with an article instead, as ordinary words: `a man`, `a woman`, `an object`, and `two men` for several. Do not use or invent a name, code word or other identifier.
- Name only the classes that fit what is visible. A picture may show one, several or none of the listed classes; describe other people, animals and objects ordinarily.
- Nothing is reserved for a trigger. Describe the subject's visible appearance, expression, clothing, pose, setting, lighting and framing in ordinary words, as the profile's format asks, so the caption reads like any caption of a picture of that class.
- Follow the profile's format and level of detail exactly. Everything else in the policy (evidence only, no speculation, no negation, no quoted text) applies unchanged.
