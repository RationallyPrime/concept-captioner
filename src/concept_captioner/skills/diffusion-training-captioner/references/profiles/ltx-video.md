# Profile: LTX video training format

Apply the concept boundary in the captioning skill to the exact selected training segment.

Write one continuous chronological paragraph. Include the visible subjects and mapped triggers,
framing, observed movement, camera changes, setting, light and real transitions. Reserve the
identity, style or defining movement assigned to the concept; describe the remaining variables.
Use the segment's own timeline, beginning at zero. Do not invent a sequence from one frame.

Integrate sound or dialogue only when the attached target supplies inspected audio. Never infer
silence from unavailable audio, or sound from visual clues. Follow the declared training
objective when audio is excluded. Describe readable signs generically without transcribing them.

Save plain caption text in the sidecar. The application's structured Caption envelope is not
part of the training text. Do not use H3 shot labels or image-reference tags for LTX.

## Fictional example

NORA7, a person in a blue jacket, crosses a sunlit courtyard from left to right, pauses beside
a wooden table and sets down a red bag. The camera follows at shoulder height, then stops as
the person turns toward the table. Pale walls and potted plants remain in the background.

This example establishes wording only, not measured training quality or checkpoint capability.
